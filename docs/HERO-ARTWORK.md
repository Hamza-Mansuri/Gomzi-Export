# Original artwork

Both assets were made with **built-in image generation**, then encoded as optimised WebP files. No API key is needed to run the website. These are private label packaging concepts, not actual product photographs.

## Hero

Files: `assets-source/images/hero-banner.png` (master original), `frontend/public/assets/images/hero-banner.webp`, `hero-banner-960.webp`.

Brief used:

> Premium Indian nutraceutical export company website hero for Gomzi Life Science LLP. Photorealistic landscape 3:2 still life of nine private label nutrition products: matte white whey and plant protein tubs with charcoal lids, protein pouch, gummy and capsule bottles, amber droppers, granules jar and slim tube. Restrained white labels with forest green and fresh lime blocks, minimal legible product names and YOUR BRAND text. Low sage plinths, warm off-white backdrop, soft laboratory glass suggestion, all products fully visible. Soft diffuse studio light, matte surfaces, grounded shadows, minimal reflection, no glossy floor, glare, blown highlights, people, watermarks or website text. Intended to sit beside live HTML headline copy.

## Sachets

File: `frontend/public/assets/images/format-sachets.webp`.

Brief used:

> Square premium nutrition product photograph: three fully visible slim white single-serve powder sachets, one upright, one leaning and one flat on a warm off-white surface with a pale sage block and a small scattering of cream powder. Restrained forest green, lime and sage curves, minimal YOUR BRAND and DAILY NUTRITION text. Matte paper foil, generous whitespace, soft diffuse lighting, no glare, reflections, people, bottles, watermarks or webpage elements.
